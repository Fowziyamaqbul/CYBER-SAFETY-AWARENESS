02 / SPOT THE SIGNAL

Know what you're
up against.

Scams often create urgency, fear, or excitement. Recognising the pattern gives you time to make a safer choice.

01 / THE IMPOSTER

Phishing & fake messages

Messages pretending to be from banks, delivery services, employers, or people you know.

RED FLAG

Unexpected links + pressure to act now

02 / THE LOCKOUT

Password attacks

Stolen or reused passwords can give attackers access to multiple accounts.

RED FLAG

One password used everywhere

03 / THE OVERSHARE

Privacy & identity risks

Personal details, live locations, and private photos can travel further than intended.

RED FLAG

Requests for sensitive information

04 / THE BAIT

Fake offers & downloads

Prize messages, job offers, and unknown files may hide fraud or malware.

RED FLAG
